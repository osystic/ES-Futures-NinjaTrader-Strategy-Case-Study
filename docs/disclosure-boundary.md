# Disclosure boundary

## Allowed in this public showcase

- anonymized problem statement;
- public-safe architecture;
- research and validation methodology;
- aggregate, non-identifying results;
- technology stack;
- evidence-backed outcome wording;
- engineering lessons and limitations.

## Excluded

- client identity or contact details;
- private conversations;
- contract, milestone or payment information;
- private repository URLs or Git history;
- NinjaScript/C# implementation source;
- raw Strategy Analyzer XML/CSV/TXT exports;
- screenshots containing machine, account or provider identifiers;
- proprietary market data or protected-news schedules;
- exact delivery archives and artifact hashes;
- credentials, tokens, endpoints or private infrastructure.

This repository has independent sanitized history and is not a fork or mirror of the confidential delivery repository.
